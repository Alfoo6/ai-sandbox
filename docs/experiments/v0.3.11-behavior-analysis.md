## Methodology and overall results

Read the saved per-step traces and aggregate the existing 484 probes. No episodes
were rerun. Coordinates are (x,y), east/south positive. Food coordinates in tables
are relative to the pre-step agent position. Decisions use only 5×5 cells and
energy; no position, clock, history, or recurrent state enters the policy. Action
order is north, south, east, west, stay; first maximum wins. Table scores are raw
outputs rounded to six decimals, not probabilities or calibrated confidence.

All three have zero blocked moves. Boundary fraction counts successful movement
**destinations** with x or y equal to 0 or 24, not time occupancy or parallel wall
following. Closer means strictly smaller minimum Manhattan distance to the same
pre-step visible food set, after actual movement; collection is a separate event.
ABA means three consecutive positions A-B-A with A≠B. A run requires at least two
consecutive overlapping reversals; stationary steps interrupt it.

The two positions are **(4,22)** and **(4,21)**. Tick 23 starts the alternating
movement; ticks 24–41 contain 18 overlapping reversals. Nine complete round trips
occur during ticks 23–40, followed by a northward move at 41. Food at **(4,20)**
is visible throughout, at offsets (0,-2) and (0,-1). No food is collected during
23–41; energy drops from 99 to 80. These are interior positions: their radius-2
observations contain no boundary cells outside the world.

Immediately before the cycle, tick 20 moves west despite adjacent northern food;
21 moves north; 22 moves east and collects food at (4,22), raising energy from
79 to 99 after the tick cost. At 22 another food at (4,20) is visible too.

At repeated visits to (4,21), the local cell pattern is unchanged and energy is
the only changing network input. South's score declines while north's rises:
at energy 82 (tick 40), south leads by 0.008808; at energy 80 (tick 42), north
leads by 0.017370. The deterministic policy therefore exits north and collects
(4,20), restoring post-step energy to 99. This directly supports an
**energy-dependent action switch**. The exact transition energy between these
observations was not measured. Alternation itself also changes food distance:
at (4,22), north remains preferred throughout the sampled cycle.

Ticks 43–45 move south with no visible food. Tick 43 returns to (4,21), producing
the episode's other, isolated ABA reversal after collection. This illustrates why
reversals alone should not be classified as errors.

The observed behavior is delayed collection, not permanent failure. “Waiting until
food offers more energy benefit” is a possible interpretation, not evidence of
intent or a learned optimal timing strategy. The cycle costs energy; whether it
improves subsequent survival requires a counterfactual, not this trace alone.

### Energy sensitivity

Open-space closer counts at energies 100,75,50,25 are respectively **11/24
(45.83%), 15/24 (62.50%), 14/24 (58.33%), 12/24 (50%)**: no monotonic improvement
with decreasing energy. Ten of 24 open-space food offsets change action across
the four energies (10/72 adjacent-energy comparisons change). Across all 112
single-food geometry/offset conditions, 24 change (24/336 adjacent comparisons).
Including no-food controls gives 27/121 changing conditions and 28/363 changed
adjacent comparisons. These counts concern argmax changes; unchanged actions do
not imply unchanged scores.

For open-space food (0,-1), energy 100 selects south while 75,50,25 select north;
food (0,-2) selects north at all four energies. This supports the trace's
energy/food-distance interaction without identifying its precise threshold.
No-food open-space actions are south, east, east, north across the four energies.

Across all food probes, 288/448 (64.29%) move closer; boundary-only food probes
account for 236/352 (67.05%). Do not interpret that difference as a boundary
benefit: feasible offsets differ between contexts. The matched action comparisons
show genuine sensitivity to boundary input. North-edge no-food behavior goes east
along the edge; south-edge behavior goes north into the interior; east-edge
behavior goes south along the edge. This is asymmetric, not a uniform wall rule.

Only **1/484** probes attempts a blocked move: southeast corner (24,24), food
(-1,0), energy 100 selects south. No stay is selected anywhere. Avoiding blocked
moves in most probes does not establish successful exploration or food collection.

## Conclusions, limitations, and future questions

Direct evidence supports energy-dependent action selection, a temporary
food-visible two-position cycle, directional asymmetry, and boundary-sensitive
choices. It does not establish deliberate waiting, a universal boundary strategy,
or that changing fitness would fix either case. The no-food episode has no
oscillation, but removes food cues and energy replenishment together and follows
a different trajectory; it is not a matched causal intervention.

One champion and selected episodes are descriptive evidence. Four energy samples,
single-food probes, and exact-edge/corner positions do not cover all reachable
observations, multi-food interactions, or near-wall distances. Scores are not
probabilities; moving closer for one step does not guarantee eventual collection.
The historical final-test seeds 1000–1099 must not guide future selection or
coefficient tuning; these retrospective cases are now development evidence.

Possible future work: replay the two local patterns with fine energy sweeps;
compare mirrored/rotated food patterns at fixed energy; extend matched probes to
one-cell-inward walls and multiple foods; compare first-collection timing and
post-collection survival on new development seeds. None was run here. The next
controlled training proposal is in [the v0.4 plan](v0.4-experiment-plan.md).
